Privacy Policy

Agentic OS Connector · Last updated 15 October 2026

Agentic OS Connector is a personal, single-user tool run by Vizonary Productions. It shows the operator's own social media statistics in a private dashboard that runs locally on the operator's computer. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses

When the account owner signs in and grants permission, the tool reads only:

  • TikTok: basic profile (open ID, username), follower count, likes count, video count, and view counts of the account's public videos.
  • Instagram: username, follower count and media count of the connected professional account.

It does not post, comment, message, or read anyone else's private data.

How data is stored

Statistics and access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to any server controlled by Vizonary Productions, and no data is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on the local computer until the operator deletes it. You can revoke access at any time in your TikTok or Instagram settings under connected apps. The tool then stops receiving data.
